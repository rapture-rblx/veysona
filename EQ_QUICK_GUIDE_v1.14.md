# Spectrum EQ 8 — Quick Guide

- **Open EQ:** select a track, show the bottom editor dock, then open the EQ area. Click `+` in the Spectrum EQ header for a larger, resizable editor.
- **Pick a band:** click its coloured circle on the graph or the matching numbered chip beneath it.
- **Edit frequency and gain:** drag the circle horizontally (frequency) or vertically (gain). Hold Shift to fine-adjust.
- **Edit width:** hover over a circle and roll the mouse wheel for Q. Shift+wheel is a finer step.
- **Bypass a band:** Alt+click its circle or right-click and use Band enabled. Use EQ On/Off on the channel strip to bypass the entire EQ.
- **Band type:** right-click a node and select Bell, Low shelf or High shelf.
- **Reset:** double-click in the graph to reset the selected band's gain; Alt+double-click resets all gains.
- **Presets:** click PRESETS in the EQ header (or FX Presets beside EQ On/Off) and choose a complete eight-band setting.
- **Copy between tracks:** PRESETS > Copy EQ from this track, select the destination, PRESETS > Paste EQ to selected track.
- **Undo/redo:** PRESETS > Undo EQ change or Redo EQ change (per selected track); EQ history is cleared when changing projects or reordering tracks.
- **Analyzer:** toggle LIVE in the EQ header. This affects only the display, not the processed audio.

The spectrum comes from a 1024-point FFT and the response curve uses the real eight-band filter math. This source release has **not yet been tested in the running Windows graphical application**.
