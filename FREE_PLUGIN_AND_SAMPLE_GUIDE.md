# Free instruments, VST3 effects and samples for Veysona

No external plugins or sound libraries are silently redistributed in this source archive. Install VST3s from their publishers and use Veysona's **Load VST3** menu to open them. Free-to-use music samples may *not* be freely redistributable as a competing sample library.

## Free VST3 instruments

- Surge XT (open-source synth): https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/downloads/ — official 64-bit Windows VST3 download. Most useful to verify third-party synth compatibility.
- Dexed (DX7-inspired FM instrument): https://github.com/asb2m10/dexed/releases — confirm the downloaded release contains a Windows VST3.
- sfizz (open-source SFZ sample player): https://github.com/sfztools/sfizz — check current releases for Windows VST3.
- Vital (free tier wavetable synth): https://vital.audio/ — free tier; review publisher's terms.

## Free VST3 audio effects

- TDR Nova (dynamic EQ): https://www.tokyodawn.net/tdr-nova/
- TDR Kotelnikov (compressor): https://www.tokyodawn.net/tdr-kotelnikov/
- Valhalla Supermassive (delay/reverb): https://valhalladsp.com/shop/reverb/valhalla-supermassive/

## Sample and orchestral instruments (licensing matters)

- Freesound: https://freesound.org/ — **filter for CC0** when content is intended to be redistributed in Veysona; CC BY assets require attribution and other licences vary.
- VSCO 2 Community Edition: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE — consult its exact sample licence before bundling.
- Spitfire LABS: https://labs.spitfireaudio.com/ — free-to-play recordings do **not** generally grant permission to redistribute its raw sampled sounds as another library. Install the instrument yourself instead.

**Built-in library in this package:** 44 original synthesis-generated samples plus 16 original single-sample instrument presets, six MIDI patterns and demos. All are included locally under `assets/`.
