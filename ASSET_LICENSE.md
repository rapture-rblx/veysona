### Veysona 1.4 original content

The 24 sample WAV files, eight keyboard presets and eight MIDI patterns prefixed `V14` are generated from scratch using `tools/generate_v14_content.py`, using mathematical oscillators, noise and algorithmic note generation. These newly generated assets are dedicated under CC0 1.0 to the extent permissible for the contributors who created them. Other legacy assets and third-party code must still be individually reviewed for public distribution.

### Veysona v1.11 procedural expansion

The 356 new `V11` WAV samples, 192 new `V11` instrument WAVs, and 48 new `V11` MIDI patterns were created by the newly included original generator `tools/generate_v111_soundbank.py` without using recorded third-party content. The 72 `.vfx.json` files are original parameter arrangements for existing processors, not licensed plugin binaries. They are made available under CC0 1.0 to the extent the project contributors own the applicable copyright. No representation is made about unrelated legacy assets.

### Veysona v1.12 new original generated assets

The 240 sample WAV files, 120 sample-root instrument WAV files, 48 MIDI patterns and 60 effects-chain preset data files prefixed `V12` are generated from mathematical oscillators, noise and MIDI events using `tools/generate_v112_soundbank.py`. No external recordings were used. The newly generated original content is dedicated to CC0 1.0 to the extent that project contributors own the applicable rights. This statement does not change older assets' rights or the project's source-code licensing.

### Veysona v1.25 original synthesized sound bank

The 1,280 `V125` WAVs are reproducibly synthesized by `tools/generate_v125_unique_bank.py` without external recordings or audio material from commercial sample libraries. They are offered under CC0 1.0 to the extent that the project contributor owns the applicable rights. Per-sample PCM hashes, names, durations and roles are recorded in `assets/V125_UNIQUE_BANK_MANIFEST.json`.

The 34 near-identical historical samples listed in `assets/V125_LEGACY_DUPLICATE_HIDES.txt` are excluded from normal browser discovery **but remain present for project compatibility**. Their existence does not change the rights or licensing status of the older bank.

The 80 `V125` effects presets are original parameter data, not third-party plugin binaries.
